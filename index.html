Starter buying guide

Three questions before you upgrade your desk

Good desk accessories should solve a real problem. Before choosing a product, ask:

  • Will it fit? Check your available desk space and device dimensions.
  • Will it work with my setup? Confirm laptop size, monitor weight or desk thickness as applicable.
  • Will I use it every day? Prioritize improvements that match your actual routine.
